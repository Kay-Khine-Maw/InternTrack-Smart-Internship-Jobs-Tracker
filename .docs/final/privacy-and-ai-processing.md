# Privacy and AI Processing

## Data minimization

InternTrack should collect only fields needed for internship preparation and
tracking. Uploaded resumes are temporary processing inputs and should not be
stored as public files.

## Ownership

Protected profile, internship, extraction-history, document and translation
operations must derive identity from the server-side session and verify
ownership before access.

## AI

AI responses are untrusted drafts. They must be parsed, normalized, reviewed and
corrected before persistence. Provider credentials remain server-side.
