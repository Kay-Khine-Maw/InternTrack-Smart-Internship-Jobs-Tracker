# InternTrack Case Study

## Problem

Internship information is scattered across job boards, documents and messages.
Students need to recover important details, correct incomplete extraction and
keep deadlines and preparation information together.

Five anonymized pre-test and post-test interview responses are now available
for U01–U05. App-task measurements, unassisted completion records and
questionnaire results remain pending.

## Process

### Discover

- Evidence source: [Anonymized interview transcripts](../discover/interview-transcripts.md)
- Participant/research codes: U01, U02, U03, U04, U05
- Main pains: scattered job information, fragmented deadline tracking,
  translation difficulty, uncertainty about skill fit/priorities and long job
  descriptions.
- Requirements changed because of evidence: preserve the review-before-save
  safeguard, improve draft-save clarity, preserve original text during
  translation and treat matching/learning recommendations as separate product
  decisions.
- Pre-test current-process findings: participants used screenshots, bookmarks,
  notes, phone reminders and Google Calendar.

### Build

InternTrack uses Next.js, server-side route handlers, Prisma, PostgreSQL and
shared UI components. Gemini is intended for structured extraction and
translation. The student reviews and corrects an editable draft before an
explicit save.
