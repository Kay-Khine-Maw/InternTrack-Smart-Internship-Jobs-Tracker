## Decisions

- Profile information, application notes, uploaded documents, extracted text, interview links, and AI prompts or responses are treated as personal data when they can identify or describe a person.
- External AI requests must contain only the minimum necessary source text and no account identifiers, tokens, or unrelated profile data.
- Future authentication, sharing, analytics, payments, chat, and signatures must not be added without applying the corresponding compliance safeguards.
