# Anonymized User-Test Interview Transcripts

## Status

Five anonymized pre-test and post-test interview responses were supplied by the
team. Moderated InternTrack sessions for **U01–U05** were run on **17 November
2026**. Task-level completion by requirement is in [session-log.md](../test/session-log.md).
Recordings are in [Software Case studies — Interview videos](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1DaICWCtuZTEzS48CV5_QI3aj0sFiQPjZ?usp=sharing).
Before/after task times were not measured.

Participant codes are used instead of names. The job descriptions below are
participant-provided contexts, not claims about the participants' identities or
employment.

## U01 — Freelance Thai Language Teacher

**Pre-test**

1. Job considered: Freelance Thai language teacher.
2. Source: Facebook groups.
3. Saving method: Saved posts and screenshots.
4. Deadline method: Phone notes.
5. Main difficulty: Some saved job links could not be found.

**Post-test**

1. Easiest: Saving job information in one place.
2. Confusing: Knowing when the draft was saved.
3. Trust: Mostly trusted the extraction, but checked the original post.
4. Review/duplicate behavior: Review and duplicate warnings helped avoid
   repeated jobs.
5. Improvement: Better organization of job opportunities.

## U02 — Thai Language Tutor

**Pre-test**

1. Job considered: Thai language tutor.
2. Source: A Thai job website.
3. Saving method: Copied the link into notes.
4. Deadline method: Google Calendar.
5. Main difficulty: Understanding formal Thai job descriptions.

**Post-test**

1. Easiest: Reading job details in English.
2. Confusing: Some translated words sounded unnatural.
3. Trust: Mostly trusted the extraction, but checked the Thai original.
4. Review/duplicate behavior: Review allowed correction of the details and
   duplicate warnings helped.
5. Improvement: Less manual translation was required.

## U05 — Data Analyst

**Pre-test**

3. Saving method: Screenshots.
4. Deadline method: Notes.
5. Main difficulty: Long job descriptions made SQL and Python requirements
   difficult to find quickly.

**Post-test**

1. Easiest: Finding important job details.
2. Confusing: Some extracted information was incomplete.

## Evidence summary

- 5/5 post-test responses described extraction as mostly trustworthy but still
  checked the original source.
- 5/5 post-test responses reported that review and/or duplicate warnings helped.
- 5/5 post-test responses described an improvement in organization,
  translation, requirement visibility or detail discovery.
- U01 reported uncertainty about when a draft was saved.
- U02 reported unnatural translation wording.
- U03 reported a need for skill matching.
- U04 reported a need for learning recommendations.
- U05 reported incomplete extracted information.

These are descriptive results from five supplied transcripts, not population
statistics. Task completion, time, error and questionnaire results require the
corresponding session and measurement records.
