# UTAUT2 Results 

## Decision

- Administer UTAUT2: Yes, as a separate questionnaire in this plan.
- Date: 17 November 2026.
- Reason: Explore perceived performance benefits, ease of use, social influence, available support, enjoyment, and intention to use independently from SUS and TAM.
- Conceptual source: Venkatesh, Thong, and Xu (2012), [Consumer Acceptance and Use of Information Technology: Extending the Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology](https://aisel.aisnet.org/misq/vol36/iss1/13/). UTAUT2 extends UTAUT with hedonic motivation, price value, and habit. 

## Scale and questionnaire

Scale: 1 = Strongly disagree; 2 = Disagree; 3 = Neither agree nor disagree; 4 = Agree; 5 = Strongly agree.

| Construct | Item 1 | Item 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Performance expectancy (PE) | InternTrack would help me organize internship opportunities effectively. | InternTrack would help me complete application preparation tasks more efficiently. |
| Effort expectancy (EE) | Learning to use InternTrack would be easy for me. | Completing my tasks in InternTrack would be straightforward. |
| Social influence (SI) | People whose opinions matter to me would encourage me to use InternTrack. | People who support my internship search would think using InternTrack is a good idea. |
| Facilitating conditions (FC) | I have access to the device and internet connection needed to use InternTrack. | I could obtain help if I encountered difficulties using InternTrack. |
| Hedonic motivation (HM) | I would enjoy using InternTrack. | Using InternTrack would be a pleasant experience. |
| Behavioral intention (BI) | I intend to use InternTrack during my internship search. | I plan to use InternTrack for future applications. |

### Constructs requiring different study conditions

- Price value (PV): N/A, which assumes a free prototype with no monetary price to evaluate. Do not substitute time savings for monetary price value. If a price is introduced, define the price scenario and use approved price-value items.
- Habit (HT): Not assessed, which assumes a first-use session. A brief first use does not establish habitual use; collect this construct after repeated use with approved items.

## Calculation method and missing-response policy

For each assessed construct, average its two responses. All items are positively worded; none are reverse scored. Average participant construct scores to obtain the group result. Round only displayed results to two decimals.
